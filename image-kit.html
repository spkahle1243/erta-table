Image Kit

for Erta Table

Bring in the list from your table

  1. In Erta Table, open the Prep tab and click Image Kit.
  2. Click Copy list, then paste it here.
  3. Generate, check each picture, and take them home as one zip.

Let Gemini connect the kit

Gemini Canvas didn’t accept any of the image models this kit asks for, which usually means Google has renamed them. Gemini knows the current ones. Click Ask Gemini (in Canvas, bottom right), paste this, and send. When it’s done, load your list again.
